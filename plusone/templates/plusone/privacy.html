Privacy and temporary access

What anonymous means here.

Other guests see your plan and match activity. A temporary identity is still backed by stored records.

Public and matched content

Your activity card is visible to visitors who can discover or open it. Messages, the shared plan and participant statuses are limited to matched guests in the product.

An anonymous guest label is not proof of a person's campus membership or real identity. Avoid publishing contact details or information you do not want another guest to read.

Stored content and AI services

The service stores temporary identities, cards, swipes, matches, messages, reports and product events. AI drafting and safety checking may send submitted content to the configured AI provider, and their inputs and results may be logged.

If you enable background browser alerts, the service stores the subscription needed to deliver them. You can turn alerts off from My updates.

Operators can access saved content when maintaining the service and handling safety reports. A private match is not a promise that operators cannot review it.

Access and retention

Your identity is tied to this browser session. Session expiry, cleared cookies, a reset or inactive-record cleanup can end access. There is no cross-device password or email recovery.

Cleanup follows the service's configured retention and processing schedule. Starting fresh closes live activity and upcoming meetups; it does not immediately delete all saved content. Safety evidence may remain longer than ordinary temporary records.

Questions or a safety concern

Use a card's report action or the report panel in your match. Reporting and blocking do not verify who a guest is.
