Help and safety

Keep your plans clear and comfortable.

You can leave a waiting room, cancel an upcoming meetup and report a concern.

Find your current plan

Return to My Plus Ones in the same browser. Open an existing match to see the current meeting point, time and cancellation status.

Agreeing confirms a plan; arrival and meetup feedback are participant self-reports. They are not verified attendance.

My Plus Ones

Meet safely

Choose a public place you know. Check the latest plan before travelling. You do not need to share contact details to use the match.

If you cannot attend, cancel the upcoming meetup from its plan. The other guest should check the latest status before travelling; browser alerts are not a guaranteed delivery channel.

Report and block

Report a public card from its details, or use the safety panel in your match. Describe the concern without sharing unnecessary personal information.

Blocking prevents further contact from that known guest identity. It cannot guarantee that the same person will not return with another temporary identity.
